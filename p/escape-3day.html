The 3-Day Escape: Coast, Islands, Nizwa

Day 1 Wadi Mibam, Sur and the turtles. Day 2 the Daymaniyat Islands. Day 3 Misfat and Nizwa. One Muscat hotel, any car.

Three day trips from one Muscat bed, no repacking: the coast and the turtles, the islands, then the mountains. The Muscat Day covers the capital itself, so do that one first. Every stop timed, every rial on the receipt.

Best forYour first 3 days in Oman
BaseOne Muscat hotel, 2 nights
CarAny car (4×4 to drive up Mibam)
Days3

Day 1: Wadi Mibam, Sur & the turtles

coast road, dhow yard, Ras Al Hadd · driving ~5.5h

08:00 Coast road east
Route 17, the sea on your left.

09:45 Wadi Mibam
The top of the Tiwi valley: emerald pools, nobody there.

13:00 Lunch on the way, or in Sur

14:00 Sur dhow yard
Ocean-going dhows still built by hand. Walk right up to the hulls.

15:00 Sur old town & the Ayjah lighthouse
Whitewashed lanes, three watchtowers, the lighthouse across the bay.

17:00 Ras Al Hadd at sunset (optional)
Where the sun rises first in Arabia. Tonight it is the sunset.

20:30 Ras Al Jinz turtle reserve (optional)
Green turtles nesting in the dark.

21:30 Back to Muscat
