The 3-Day Muscat & Wadis Escape

The long weekend that covers the classics.

One day for the capital, one for Wadi Tiwi and the Sur coast, one for Nizwa and the mountains. First-timer proof, any car, one hotel, no repacking. Shares no days with the 1-day plan: do both back to back and nothing repeats.

Best forFirst-timers
BaseOne Muscat hotel
CarAny car
Days3

Day 1: Muscat

mosque, souq, forts, corniche · driving ~1h · ≈ OMR 3 pp

08:00 Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque
Visitor window closes 11:00. Free; robe hire 2.5 if you need one.

11:30 Lunch in Old Muscat
Bait Al Luban on the harbour is the one worth booking.

13:00 Al Alam Palace + Jalali & Mirani forts
Viewed from outside, 45 minutes of the best photo spots in the capital.

16:00 Mutrah Souq + fort
The souq wakes up late afternoon. Frankincense, silver, karak.

18:00 Mutrah Corniche at dusk
The postcard walk. Dinner idea: Al Bait Al Omani.

Day 2: Wadi Tiwi & the Sur coast

the coast beyond Shab · driving ~4.5h · entries free

08:00 Coast road east
Breakfast on the way. Same road as the famous wadis, different prizes.

10:00 Wadi Tiwi
Nine villages stacked up a canyon you can drive into, pools a fraction as busy as Shab next door.

13:00 Lunch in Sur
Grilled fish by the lagoon.

14:00 Sur dhow yard
Ocean-going dhows still built by hand, walk right up to the hulls.

15:00 Sur old town & Ayjah
Whitewashed lanes, three watchtowers, the lighthouse across the bay.

16:30 White Beach on the way home
The swim stop.

18:30 Back to Muscat

Day 3: Nizwa + the mountain village

souq, fort, old quarters · driving ~4h · ≈ OMR 5 pp

08:00 Drive to Nizwa
1h45 on smooth highway.

10:00 Nizwa Souq
Pottery, silver, dates. Friday morning adds the goat market.

11:30 Harat Al Aqr
The restored old quarter behind the fort.

12:30 Nizwa Fort
OMR 5, the best fort in the country.

14:30 Misfat Al Abriyeen
Mud houses, falaj, date terraces. Walk it slowly.

17:00 Back to Muscat
Two hours, dinner in the city.
