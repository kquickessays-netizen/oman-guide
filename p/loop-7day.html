The 7-Day Ultimate Oman Loop

Mountains, wadis, desert and coast, the full circle.

The route I'd drive if I had one week and one shot: capital, coast, turtles at midnight, a night in the dunes, Nizwa, the canyon rim, and home through the mountain villages. The 1, 3 and 5-day plans never repeat each other; this one is allowed to, it is the best of everything stitched into a single moving loop.

Best forThe full picture, one week
BaseMoving loop, 6 nights
Car4×4 recommended
Days7

Day 1: Land & the capital

mosque, corniche, souq · driving ~1h · entries free

09:00 Grand Mosque
If you landed early enough, visitor window shuts 11:00. Otherwise slot it before the airport on day 7.

12:00 Settle in, eat, rest
Long-haul landing day, don't overplan it.

17:00 Mutrah Corniche at dusk
The postcard walk as the heat drops.

18:30 Mutrah Souq
Frankincense and silver as the lanterns come on.

20:00 Dinner at Bait Al Luban
Omani classics over the harbour.

Day 2: The coast road

sinkhole + the famous wadi · driving ~3h · ≈ OMR 1 pp

08:00 Coast road east
Route 17, the sea on your left.

10:00 Bimmah Sinkhole
An hour, no more.

11:30 Wadi Shab
Boat, canyon, swim into the waterfall cave. Give it the whole afternoon.

16:00 Drive on toward Sur
45 minutes down the coast.

Day 3: Sur & the turtles

dhow yard, then midnight nests · driving ~2h · ≈ OMR 10 pp

09:30 Sur dhow yard
An hour among hand-built ocean-going hulls.

11:00 Sur old town & Ayjah
Whitewashed lanes and the lighthouse across the bay.

13:00 Lunch by the lagoon
Grilled fish town.

15:00 Beach hours at Ras Al Hadd
The first sunrise in Arabia is here, the afternoon is all yours.

21:00 Ras Al Jinz turtle tour
Green turtles nesting in the dark. Book direct with the reserve, numbers are capped: entry ~3, tour ~7.

Day 4: Into the sands

dunes, camels, stars · driving ~3h · ≈ OMR 35 pp

09:00 Inland to Al Wasil
2.5 hrs, smooth tarmac.

13:00 Meet your camp at the sand's edge
They drive the dunes, you don't. Half board included in most camps.

16:30 Camels, then the sunset ridge
The photo you came for.

20:00 Dinner, stars, no signal

Day 5: Bani Khalid & Nizwa

the oasis, then the fort · driving ~3.5h · ≈ OMR 5 pp

08:30 Out of the sand
The camp tows you back to tarmac.

10:00 Wadi Bani Khalid
Pools from the car park, walk 15 minutes upstream and the crowd is gone.

13:00 Lunch en route
Ibra or the highway grills.

15:00 Nizwa Souq + Fort
Pottery and dates below, then climb the round tower before it closes.

Day 6: The canyon rim

Jabal Shams & the Balcony Walk · driving ~2.5h · entries free

07:00 Up the Jabal Shams road
1h45 from Nizwa; the last stretch is graded track, take it slow.

09:00 The Balcony Walk
8.7km out and back along the rim, W6 markers, 4 to 5 hours. Oman's best walk.

14:30 Late lunch at the top
Jacket on, you are at 2,000m.

16:00 Wadi Ghul from below
The same canyon from the floor, 1,000m walls both sides.

Day 7: Villages, the cave, home

the loop closes · driving ~3h · ≈ OMR 7 pp

09:00 Misfat Al Abriyeen
Mud houses and the falaj in morning light, walk it slowly.

11:30 Al Hoota Cave
The cool hour underground. Book the slot ahead.

13:00 Lunch in Al Hamra
Under the palms of the old quarter.

14:00 The easy run home
Two hours back to Muscat. Fly out tonight or tomorrow.
