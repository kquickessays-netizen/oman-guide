The Muscat Day

The capital done properly in one day. No 4x4, barely any driving, home for dinner.

Mosque at nine while it is still calm, the opera house, then Mutrah for the whole afternoon — fish market, lunch, halwa, souq, fort — finishing at the palace when the light goes gold. The exact day from my reel, with the drive between each stop and what it costs. Any car does it, and most of the afternoon is on foot.

the capital, in the right order · driving ~1h · from OMR 12 pp

09:00 Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque
Start here, before the heat and the coaches. Take your time — the detail is the whole point, and the calm at this hour is half of why it works. Visitor hours end at 11:00; shoulders and knees covered, and there is robe hire at the gate if you need it.

10:30 Royal Opera House
Twenty minutes across town. Elegance on another level, and the guided tour is the only way to see inside — do it, it is worth the hour. Visits run Saturday to Thursday, 08:30–17:30, so this is a weekday plan; entry is 3 excluding tax.

12:30 Mutrah fish market
Twenty-five minutes to the corniche. Aim for the free spots beside the fish market itself — they are the only free ones and they go early; everywhere else along the corniche is paid parking, by phone. The market is raw, local and completely unstaged, so walk through even if you buy nothing.

13:15 Lunch at Bait Al Luban
Traditional Omani food with the harbour in the window. Worth booking, it fills at lunch.

14:15 Halwa and kahwa on the corniche
Walk the water, then stop for Omani halwa and coffee — you are handed both to try before anyone mentions buying.

14:45 Mutrah Souq
Go in without a plan and get lost. Frankincense, silver, textiles. Haggle gently, expect kahwa, and keep an eye on the time.

15:30 Matrah Fort
Climb it. One of the best views in Muscat — the corniche end to end, the harbour, and the dhows from above. Entry is 3 excluding tax.

16:15 Al Alam Palace
A short drive round the headland to finish: the palace and the two Portuguese forts on the cliffs above it. Viewed from outside, and the reason it is last is the light.
