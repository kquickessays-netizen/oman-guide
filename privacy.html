Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Exploring Oman (exploresoman.com) is a free travel guide to Oman. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: the guide works without an account, we collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

The site is operated by Mustakshif Marketing and Digital Content (مستكشف للتسويق والمحتوى الرقمي), commercial registration 1677902, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: exploree.oman@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you just use the guide

  • On your device only: the places you save (♥), the places you tick as visited (✓) and your explorer rank are stored in your own browser. They are not sent to us unless you create an account.
  • Anonymous usage statistics: which pages and tabs you open and which buttons you tap (for example a WhatsApp or car-rental button). These are tied to a random device ID kept in your browser (in local storage and a first-party cookie called oman_device_id, which lasts up to 400 days), together with your device type, operating system, browser, screen size, language and time zone. The ID identifies a browser, not a person. We don't use advertising trackers.

If you ask us to plan your trip or contact us

  • The name, email or WhatsApp number, travel dates, group size and notes you type into the trip-planning form, so we can reply and prepare your quote and plan.
  • If you message us on WhatsApp, the conversation is also handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy.

If you join the email list

  • Your email address, used only to tell you when the guide is updated.

If you post a review

  • Your star rating, the name you give and your text. Reviews are published publicly on that place's page.

If you create an account

  • Your email address, and your password if you choose one. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • The name you give and, if you choose one, the country you're from.
  • Your saved and visited places, so they follow you to any device.
  • For each device you sign in on: device type, operating system, browser, language, time zone and when it was last used.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Continue with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We don't ask for, and can't access, anything else in your Google account, such as your contacts, Gmail or Drive.

We use this information only to create your account, sign you in, greet you by name and credit any review you decide to post. We don't use it for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that run the site for us.

Exploring Oman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove Exploring Oman's access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

How we use it

  • To run the guide and your account, and to sync your saves between devices.
  • To reply to you, and to prepare trip quotes and plans you ask for.
  • To send guide updates, only if you joined the email list.
  • To show your review on the place you reviewed.
  • To understand which parts of the guide people use, so we can improve it.

We don't sell or rent personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of providers to run the site. They process data on our behalf, or when your browser loads something from them, which means they can see your IP address:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in system (accounts, reviews, trip requests, statistics).
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Google: Google sign-in (if you use it) and Google Fonts.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles; jsDelivr and unpkg: code libraries.
  • Affiliate partners such as Discover Cars: only if you tap through to them. They set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Trip requests, reviews and email-list sign-ups are kept for as long as they are useful for the purpose above, and deleted on request at any time.

Your choices

  • See, correct or delete your data, or delete your account: email exploree.oman@gmail.com from the address on your account. We'll do it within 30 days.
  • Email list: reply to any email and we'll remove you.
  • On-device data: clearing your browser's data for this site removes your local saves and the device ID.

Children

The guide is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

If you face an issue or something is incorrect, please contact us and we will make the necessary adjustments.
