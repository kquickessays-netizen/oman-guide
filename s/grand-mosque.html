Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque

The one thing every visitor should do in Muscat.

The mosque that changes how people see the whole country, the scale, the light, the second-largest hand-woven carpet on earth. Free to enter, and an hour well spent.

How to get to Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque

  1. Short taxi or drive from anywhere in Muscat. Visitor parking on site.
  2. Non-Muslim visiting hours: 8–11am, every day except Friday and public holidays.
  3. Be there by 10am at the latest, or you're walking straight back out.
  4. Dress code is enforced: long sleeves, long trousers/skirt, and a headscarf for women.

What you'll do

  1. Start in the courtyard.
  2. Then the main prayer hall, the chandelier and the carpet are the reason people talk about this place.
  3. Give it an hour. Don't rush the prayer hall; it's the whole point.

Tips from a local guide

  • Open to visitors 8–11am, every day except Friday and public holidays. That's a narrow window, plan the morning around it, not the other way round.
  • Go right at 8am, noticeably quieter and cooler for photos.
  • Pair it with Mutrah Souq / the Corniche the same morning.

What to bring

  • Arms and legs covered
  • Women: a scarf to cover your hair
  • Shoes you can slip off easily

Good to have

  • Robe hire on site (~OMR 2.5) if you forget
  • Wide lens for the prayer hall
  • Arrive at 8am, empty and cool
