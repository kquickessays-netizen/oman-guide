Wadi Al Khoudh

Muscat's own wadi, one of the closest to drive to.

The wadi everyone in Muscat drives over and almost nobody drives into. After rain, the whole city comes to watch it flow. It's special for the locals, who combine it with tea, or grilling sometimes, while they enjoy the weather.

DifficultyEasy–moderate
Time needed2 hrs
SwimNo, the water is too shallow
Vehicle4×4 for the wadi bed; 2WD parks at the edge

How to get to Wadi Al Khoudh

  1. Minutes from Al Khoud village: through the mountains, past the village, park at the lot if you're in a 2WD.
  2. Experienced 4×4 drivers can carry on up the wadi bed, carefully.

What you'll do

  1. Walk the valley and the mountains around it.
  2. Do it like the locals: bring lunch or a grill and tea, and make an afternoon of it.
  3. Best just after mild rain, when the wadi actually flows.

Wadi Al Khoudh: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wadi Al Khoudh?

Oct–Apr, never after rain upstream. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Wadi Al Khoudh?

2 hrs.

How long is the walk at Wadi Al Khoudh?

20–40 min up the gorge, some boulder-hopping.

Can you swim at Wadi Al Khoudh?

Yes. 1 hr in the upper pools.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wadi Al Khoudh?

Yes, the last stretch needs a 4x4.

Is Wadi Al Khoudh good for kids?

Yes, it works with children.
