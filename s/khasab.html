Khasab & its castle

Musandam's capital, guarded by a 17th-century Portuguese fort.

The gateway town to the fjords. Khasab Castle is small, restored and genuinely good, and the town is where every dhow, ferry and mountain safari starts. Reaching it is the adventure: fly from Muscat, take the Shinas ferry, or drive through the UAE with your passport.
