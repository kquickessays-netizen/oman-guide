National Museum of Oman

The country's story, told properly. Do it on day one.

Opposite the Sultan's palace in Old Muscat, the National Museum lays out five thousand years, seafaring, forts, silver, dhows, Islamic art, in a building that's a pleasure to be in. An hour here makes every fort and souq afterwards make more sense.
