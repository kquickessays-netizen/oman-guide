Wahiba Sands

A night in the dunes you won't get back home.

Tarmac to Al Wasil, then 4×4 into the dunes: dune bashing, camels, sunset on a ridge, then stars. Book the camp ahead and bring a warm layer, it gets cold at night.

How to get to Wahiba Sands

  1. Trips start from Bidiyah, about 2 hrs from Muscat, then 20 minutes into the dunes.
  2. Book the camp or operator before you leave. In a 2WD you stop at their pickup point; in a 4×4 you follow them in.

What you'll do

  1. A sunset tour runs about 2 hours, but the packages go from a short trip to multiple days, it depends on the camp you book.
  2. Dune bashing, sandboarding, camels, the fire and the stars, pick your package.

Tips from a local guide

  • Go Oct–Mar. Summer in the desert is genuinely dangerous heat.
  • Ask for a camp away from the main road, that's the difference between stars and no stars.
  • It gets cold at night. Nobody believes me, and everybody regrets it.

What to bring

  • A warm layer, the desert gets genuinely cold at night
  • Scarf for the sand
  • Your camp booking

Good to have

  • Tripod for the stars
  • Sandals you don't mind losing to a dune
  • Torch
  • A book, the afternoon is slow

Wahiba Sands: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wahiba Sands?

Oct–Mar. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Wahiba Sands?

Overnight (1–2 nights).

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wahiba Sands?

Yes, the last stretch needs a 4x4.

Is Wahiba Sands good for kids?

Yes, it works with children.
