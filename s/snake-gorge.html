Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)

The most adrenaline you'll find in an Omani wadi.

Cliff jumps, swims through narrow gorges and proper scrambling. Spectacular, but it bites if you don't know it. Go with a guide, no exceptions.

DifficultyHard, real canyoning
Time needed~6 hrs + drive
Hike3–5 hrs through the gorge
SwimYes, jumps and abseils; distance varies by route, with deep sections
Vehicle4×4 required (mountain road)
Best seasonOct–Apr
GuideRequired

How to get to Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)

  1. Drive through Wadi Bani Awf to the Snake Gorge trailhead.
  2. Park at the designated area near the canyon entrance.
  3. Go with a professional canyoning guide carrying proper safety gear for the day's conditions. This is a technical canyon: no guide, no entry. Don't risk it.

What you'll do

  1. Hike one of Oman's narrowest slot canyons.
  2. Swim deep, clear pools and scramble the boulders between them.
  3. Climb rock obstacles and small waterfalls on the guide's ropes.
  4. The walls tower the whole way and the water stays turquoise to the end.

Tips from a local guide

  • Only jump where your guide says, depths change with every flood.
  • Helmet + good shoes. This is canyoning.
  • Never in or after rain. Ever.

What to bring

  • A guide with ropes and helmets, do not do this alone
  • Shoes that grip wet rock
  • Dry bag
  • Water

Good to have

  • Wetsuit top in winter, the water is genuinely cold in the narrows
  • Chin-strap for anything on your head
  • Nothing loose in pockets, you will lose it

Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf): questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)?

Oct–Apr. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)?

~6 hrs + drive.

How long is the walk at Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)?

~6 hrs through the gorge, this is canyoning, not hiking.

Can you swim at Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)?

Yes. Continuous. Cliff jumps (3–4m) and two ~20m abseils in the upper section.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf)?

Yes, the last stretch needs a 4x4.

Is Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf) good for kids?

It's better for adults and older teens who are comfortable with the walk and the swim.
