Daymaniyat Islands

Oman's best reef, a boat ride from the city.

A protected marine reserve 18km off Seeb, turtles, rays, reef, and a real chance of something bigger. Half a day, and the best snorkelling in the country.

Best forSnorkelling / diving
Time neededHalf day
SwimYes, the whole point
Best seasonNov–Mar (visibility, turtles)
Booking2–3 days ahead in peak season
PermitEnvironment Authority, via your operator
⚠️ May–OctBeach landing banned (turtle nesting), boat + water only
