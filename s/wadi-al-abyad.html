Wadi Al Abyad

The white wadi, milky-blue pools an hour from Muscat.

Mineral springs feed a chain of pools with a pale, milky-blue tint you won't see in any other wadi, that's the 'abyad' (white). Short flat walk-in, palms, and far fewer people than the famous names. One of the best low-effort wadi days near the capital.

DifficultyEasy to moderate
Time neededHalf day from Muscat
SwimOptional, depends on rain and season; you can walk past the pools unless it's flooded
Vehicle4×4 recommended for the wadi track
Best seasonOct–Apr
EntryFree. A guide is recommended for the driving
