Wadi Hawer

For people who want an adventure, not a viewpoint.

A full day: 4+ hours of scrambling and boulder-hopping, wet most of it, 2WD to the start. Remote and technical enough that you go with a guide.

How to get to Wadi Hawer

  1. Book a guide first, DM me for my recommendation.
  2. Any car reaches the meet-up point, about 2.5 hrs from Muscat, good views the whole way.

What you'll do

  1. Safety briefing, then the guide leads with the ropes and gear: climbing, scrambling, pool after pool, waterfalls along the way.
  2. Real shoes and real care, there are dangerous sections.
  3. The waterfall in the cave near the end is the highlight.

Wadi Hawer: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wadi Hawer?

Oct–Apr. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Wadi Hawer?

Full day.

How long is the walk at Wadi Hawer?

Full day, 6+ hrs of scrambling and boulder-hopping.

Can you swim at Wadi Hawer?

Yes. Lots. You will be wet most of the day.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wadi Hawer?

Yes, the last stretch needs a 4x4.

Is Wadi Hawer good for kids?

It's better for adults and older teens who are comfortable with the walk and the swim.
