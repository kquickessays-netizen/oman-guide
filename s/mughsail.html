Mughsail Beach

Blowholes, cliffs and a beach that doesn't look like the rest of Oman.

40 minutes west of Salalah on good tarmac, any car. The blowholes at the western end need a decent swell: flat sea and nothing happens. In khareef the cliffs behind turn green.
