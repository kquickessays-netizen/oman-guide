Nizwa Friday goat market

Sunrise, one auction ring, and half the interior's livestock.

Every Friday before the heat, herders circle goats and cattle around a ring of buyers at Nizwa's souq, bidding as they walk. It's loud, it's real, it's been happening for centuries, and visitors are welcome to stand in the middle of it. The best free cultural show in Oman.

Best forThe real Oman, no ticket
WhenFridays only, ~6:30–9am, earlier is better
Time needed1–2 hrs + the souq after
EntryFree
