Ras Al Jinz

One of the rarest turtle experiences globally — watch sea turtle species only found in Oman.

Oman's famous turtle nesting beach at the easternmost tip of Arabia. On a guided night or dawn visit you watch green turtles haul ashore to lay eggs, and hatchlings scramble for the sea.

How to get to Ras Al Jinz

  1. 3 hrs from Muscat, any car.
  2. Book the reserve's hotel ahead: the night tours only run for guests, and confirm the turtle tour is included.

What you'll do

  1. The tour leaves the hotel at night under red lights: huge greens digging nest pits and laying eggs.
  2. With luck you'll catch a hatch, baby turtles running for the water.
  3. The museum fills in the science.

Tips from a local guide

  • Book well ahead, they cap numbers and it sells out.
  • No flash. Ever. It disorients them and they'll turn back to sea without laying.
  • It's cold on that beach at night. Bring a jacket.

What to bring

  • Booking confirmation, numbers are capped
  • Closed shoes for soft sand in the dark
  • A jacket, it's cold on that beach at night

Good to have

  • Red-light torch (never white light near turtles)
  • NO flash photography, it disorients them
  • Patience
