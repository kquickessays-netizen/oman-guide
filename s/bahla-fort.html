Bahla Fort

The UNESCO one, Oman's greatest mud-brick fortress.

The only fort in Oman on the UNESCO World Heritage list, and it earns it: a vast mud-brick citadel rising over a 12km ring of ancient walls, restored over decades. Twenty minutes from Nizwa, do them in one day with Jabrin.
