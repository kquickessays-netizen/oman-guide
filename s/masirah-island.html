Masirah Island

Oman's big wild island: ferries, empty coasts, four turtle species.

A car-ferry ride off the east coast, Masirah is where Oman keeps its emptiest beaches, its kitesurf season and some of the world's densest turtle nesting. Take the vehicle ferry from Shannah, stay a night or three, do laps of the island.
