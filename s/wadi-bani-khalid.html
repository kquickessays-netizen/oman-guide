Wadi Bani Khalid

The face of wadis in Oman.

The pools start minutes' walk from the car park: no hike, any car, 2.5 hrs from Muscat on Route 23. Walk 15–20 min upstream and the crowds are gone.

How to get to Wadi Bani Khalid

  1. Route 23 inland from Muscat, 2.5 hrs, asphalt the whole way, any car.
  2. (Route 17 along the coast is prettier but closer to 4 hrs. Your call.)
  3. Park in the big lot closest to the pools. No 4×4 needed.

What you'll do

  1. Skip the first pools by the car park, that's where everyone stops.
  2. Walk 15–20 min upstream. The crowds thin out and the water gets better.
  3. Keep going and there's a cave further in, if you want more than a swim.

Tips from a local guide

  • Walk 15 min past the busy pools and you'll have it to yourself.
  • Dress modestly, it's right next to a village.
  • Wadi Hawer is close by, pair them if you have the legs.

What to bring

  • Modest swimwear, it's beside a village
  • Water shoes
  • Water

Good to have

  • Goggles
  • Towel
  • Coins for the toilets (400 baisa) and changing tents (100 baisa)
  • Picnic, there are shaded spots upstream

Wadi Bani Khalid: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wadi Bani Khalid?

Year-round, Oct–Apr the best. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How much does Wadi Bani Khalid cost?

Free.

How long do you need at Wadi Bani Khalid?

2–4 hrs.

How long is the walk at Wadi Bani Khalid?

None to the main pools, 15–20 min upstream to escape the crowds.

Can you swim at Wadi Bani Khalid?

Yes. As long as you like, this is a swimming day.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wadi Bani Khalid?

Any car (2WD ok).

Is Wadi Bani Khalid good for kids?

Yes, it works with children.
