Sifah Beach

The easy wild beach an hour from town — with a famous surprise in the shallows.

The beach at Al Sifah: long sand, clear water, mountains behind, an hour from Muscat. And some days, baby reef sharks cruise the shallows at your feet — harmless, skittish and completely mesmerising (yes, the reel was filmed here).

How to get to Sifah Beach

  1. Head for Al Sifah, about an hour from Muscat on a good sealed road — any car.
  2. ⚠️ The final sandy stretch swallows 2WDs: without a 4×4, stop where the hard ground ends and walk the last part. With one, drop tyre pressure a little and carry on.
  3. Walk the shore away from the busiest section and watch the shallows — that's where the small sharks patrol.

What you'll do

  1. Wade in slowly and stand still, the small sharks come to you.
  2. They're baby reef sharks, curious and harmless, let them pass, don't chase or corner them.
  3. Swim properly when they've moved on, the water here is glass on a calm day.

Tips from a local guide

  • Still feet beat splashing, the sharks spook easily.
  • Never grab or block a shark, look, film, let them be.
  • Early morning light makes them easiest to spot.

What to bring

  • Water
  • Sun protection, no shade

Good to have

  • Polarised sunglasses, you'll spot the sharks sooner
  • Snorkel for the calm days

Sifah Beach: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Sifah Beach?

Oct–May. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Sifah Beach?

Half day with the drive.

Can you swim at Sifah Beach?

Yes. An hour or two, the sharks come and go.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Sifah Beach?

Any car to Sifah — the sandy stretch itself is 4×4 only.

Is Sifah Beach good for kids?

Yes, it works with children.
