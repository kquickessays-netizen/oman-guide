The dhow yard at Sur

Watch thousand-year-old shipbuilding happen in front of you.

Sur's working dhow yard still builds and restores ocean-going wooden ships by hand, teak hulls, no drawings, knowledge passed father to son. You walk among the hulls and watch the work. One of the last places on Earth to see it.
