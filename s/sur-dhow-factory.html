The dhow yard at Sur

Watch thousand-year-old shipbuilding happen in front of you.

Sur's working dhow yard still builds and restores ocean-going wooden ships by hand, teak hulls, no drawings, knowledge passed father to son. You walk among the hulls and watch the work. One of the last places on Earth to see it.

Best forLiving heritage
Time needed30–60 min
WhenMornings, when work is on
EntryFree, with a paid museum right beside it. A polite hello to the carpenters is the real ticket
VehicleAny car

How to get to The dhow yard at Sur

  1. On the lagoon edge at the entrance to Sur, signposted, park beside it.

What you'll do

  1. Wander between hulls in every stage of build, keels to nearly-launched.
  2. Watch the carpenters work teak with hand tools, ask before you photograph the men, they usually say yes.
  3. The giant ceremonial dhow by the roundabout makes the scale point for you.
