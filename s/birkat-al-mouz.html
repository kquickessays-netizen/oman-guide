Birkat Al Mouz

Ruins, banana groves and a UNESCO falaj, the gateway to Jabal Akhdar.

At the foot of the Jabal Akhdar road: the abandoned mud-brick quarter of Harat Al Sibani stacked above banana plantations, with Falaj Al Khatmeen, one of Oman's five UNESCO-listed aflaj, running through it. Twenty minutes, or two hours if the light is good.
