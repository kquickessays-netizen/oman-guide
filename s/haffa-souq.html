Al Haffa Souq

Frankincense by the scoop, a street back from the sea.

The old frankincense souq near the corniche, sacks of resin graded by colour, bakhoor, Dhofari incense burners, and the smell that tells you you're in Salalah and nowhere else. Come at dusk when the town wakes up.
