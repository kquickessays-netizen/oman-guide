Pebble Beach, Tiwi

The relaxed stop on the Tiwi coast everyone drives straight past.

Smooth stones instead of sand, clear water, and the Sur coast's cliffs behind you. Minutes from Wadi Shab and Wadi Tiwi, it's the easy decompression stop after a wadi morning, and next door hides the Romantic Cave.

How to get to Pebble Beach, Tiwi

  1. It's on the coast by Tiwi, right off Route 17, 5–10 min from the Wadi Shab bridge.
  2. Park above the beach and walk down, any car.

What you'll do

  1. Swim off the pebbles, the water is usually clearer here than on sand beaches.
  2. Walk the shoreline toward the cliffs, that's where the Romantic Cave hides.
  3. Watch the fishing boats work the bay.

Pebble Beach, Tiwi: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Pebble Beach, Tiwi?

Oct–Apr. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at Pebble Beach, Tiwi?

1–2 hrs.

Can you swim at Pebble Beach, Tiwi?

Yes. An easy hour.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Pebble Beach, Tiwi?
