Wadi Mibam

The most beautiful wadi in Oman — the top of the Wadi Tiwi valley.

The crown of the Wadi Tiwi valley, up at Mibam village: fifteen minutes down from the parking to the first waterfall, then two or three pools you swim between. The last stretch of track is 4×4 only. The lower valley — the drive itself — is Wadi Tiwi's own entry.
