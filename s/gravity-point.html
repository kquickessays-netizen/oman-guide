Gravity Point (Anti-Gravity Road)

Put the car in neutral. It rolls uphill. Argue about why on the drive back.

A stretch of the Ittin road where a stopped car in neutral appears to roll up the slope. It's an optical illusion, the horizon lies to you, but nobody believes that until they've tried it. Twenty minutes of pure fun on the way to the plateau.
