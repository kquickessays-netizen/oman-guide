Matrah Fort & the corniche

The little fort with the best free view in old Muscat.

The 16th-century Portuguese fort on the rock above Mutrah harbour. Climb up for the view every postcard of Oman is trying to be, then spend the evening below it: corniche, fish market, souq. This is 'things to do in Matrah' in one entry.
