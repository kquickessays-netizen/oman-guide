Wadi Shab

The famous one, and for once the hype is real.

A 45–60 min walk/hike from the boat drop past a string of pools. The last 20 minutes is swim-only: through a narrow gap into a cave with a waterfall inside it.

DifficultyModerate

How to get to Wadi Shab

  1. Drive Route 17 south from Muscat toward Sur/Tiwi, 1h40 to 2h.
  2. Park at the free lot under the bridge. It's small: on a weekend, be there before 9am.
  3. Take the boat across, 2 minutes, 1 OMR pp return, pay as you board. Last boat ~5pm.
  4. Walk in from the far bank: 45–60 min, mostly flat, along the wadi bed.

What you'll do

  1. Follow the path past a chain of turquoise pools, crossing the wadi bed a few times.
  2. Swim the last stretch, there is no dry route around it. Phone in a dry bag.
  3. Squeeze through the narrow gap in the rock at the end.
  4. Inside: a hidden cave with a waterfall in it. That's the reason you came.

Tips from a local guide

  • Go early, it fills up by mid-morning.
  • A dry bag is essential to get a phone to the cave.
  • Leave valuables behind, you swim the last part.
  • With a guide along, ask to climb the waterfall, it's the full version of the wadi.

What to bring

  • Water shoes (grippy, drain fast)
  • Dry bag, there is no dry route to the cave
  • 2L water per person
  • Sun hat

Good to have

  • Goggles or a mask, the cave is worth seeing underwater
  • GoPro / waterproof phone case
  • Waterproof head torch for the cave
  • Small towel
  • Cash for the boat (~OMR 1)

Wadi Shab: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wadi Shab?

Oct–Apr. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How much does Wadi Shab cost?

~OMR 1 pp (incl. boat).

How long do you need at Wadi Shab?

Half day (4–5 hrs).

How long is the walk at Wadi Shab?

45–60 min each way from the boat drop.

Can you swim at Wadi Shab?

Yes. 1–2 hrs, the last 200m to the cave is swim-only.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wadi Shab?

Any car + 2-min boat crossing.

Is Wadi Shab good for kids?

It's better for adults and older teens who are comfortable with the walk and the swim.
