Wadi Shab

The famous one, and for once the hype is real.

A flat 45–60 min walk from the boat drop past a string of pools. The last 20 minutes is swim-only: through a narrow gap into a cave with a waterfall inside it.

DifficultyModerate
Time neededHalf day (4–5 hrs)
Hike45–60 min each way
SwimYes, ~20 min into the cave
VehicleAny car + 2-min boat crossing
Best seasonOct–Apr
Entry fee~OMR 1 pp (incl. boat)
