Rose season on Jabal Akhdar

Three weeks a year, the Green Mountain turns pink.

From roughly late March to late April the terrace villages of Jabal Akhdar bloom with damask roses, and the air genuinely smells of them. Families pick at dawn and distil rosewater the way they have for centuries. Miss the window, wait a year.

Best forThe bloom + rosewater houses
Season~Late March to late April, short and sharp
WhereAl Ayn, Al Aqr & Ash Shirayjah terraces
Time neededHalf day on the mountain
Vehicle4×4 required for the Jabal Akhdar road (checkpoint enforced)
EntryFree, distillery visits vary
