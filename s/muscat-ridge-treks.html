The Mutrah ridge treks

Real mountain trails that start where the city parking ends.

Muscat is one of the few capitals where marked treks leave from the corniche: the C38 climbs from Riyam to the ridgeline above Mutrah harbour, and its sister paths (Sidab coastal, the Geotrek) thread the same bare hills. Two hours, city shoes optional, views you'd fly for.

How to get to The Mutrah ridge treks

  1. Park at Riyam Park on the Mutrah corniche.
  2. The C38 trailhead is signposted behind the park, yellow-red-yellow waymarks.
  3. Finish in Mutrah and walk the corniche back to the car.

What you'll do

  1. Climb out of the palms into bare rock ridges in about twenty minutes.
  2. Top out with the whole harbour, the forts and the ocean below you.
  3. Descend into the old town and reward yourself in the souq.

Tips from a local guide

  • Start at first light, the rock throws heat by 9am even in winter.
  • Waymarks fade in places: if you've lost paint for 5 minutes, backtrack.
  • Never in summer afternoons. This trail has caught people out.
  • Good shoes, and company: this one is better with a guide.

What to bring

  • Real shoes, polished rock and loose stone
  • 1.5L water pp
  • Sun cover

Good to have

  • Head torch if you're chasing sunset
  • Trekking poles

The Mutrah ridge treks: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit The Mutrah ridge treks?

Oct–Mar, early. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How long do you need at The Mutrah ridge treks?

Half a morning.

How long is the walk at The Mutrah ridge treks?

2–3 hrs for the classic Riyam → Mutrah line.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach The Mutrah ridge treks?

None needed, start at Riyam Park.

Is The Mutrah ridge treks good for kids?

It's better for adults and older teens who are comfortable with the walk.
