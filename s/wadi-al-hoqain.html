Wadi Al Hoqain, the trenches

Sulfur-blue water in carved rock channels, and it's easy if you can swim.

Long, straight rock trenches filled with pale-blue, sulfur-rich water, south of Rustaq. No serious hike, no scramble, you walk in, float and swim down the channels, and wonder why nobody told you about it sooner. From my wadi series: one of the easiest in terms of hiking, but it would definitely need a decent swim.

DifficultyMedium
Time neededHalf day from Muscat
SwimYes, calm channels
