Wadi Darbat

Waterfalls, green meadows and camels in the mist.

In khareef season this valley turns into something that shouldn't exist in Arabia: waterfalls pouring off a travertine cliff, lakes, mist, and camels grazing on actual grass. The rest of the year it's a calm green valley with a lake and boat rides, still the first place I'd send anyone in Dhofar.
