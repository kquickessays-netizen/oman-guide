Wadi Tanuf

A ruined village, a gorge, and almost nobody there.

Palm groves, turquoise pools under small waterfalls, and the bombed-out ruins of old Tanuf village at the mouth of the gorge. Half an hour from Nizwa and a fraction of the traffic.

How to get to Wadi Tanuf

  1. 30 min from Nizwa on Route 21, signposted to Tanuf.
  2. 2WD reaches the ruins and the wadi mouth.
  3. The dirt track deeper in is rough, that part wants a 4×4.

What you'll do

  1. Start at the ruins of old Tanuf, bombed out in the 1950s and left standing.
  2. Walk up into the gorge: palm groves, falaj channels, turquoise pools under small waterfalls.
  3. Water's up? Swim. Water's down? Still one of the better short gorge walks in the country.
  4. Either way it's usually empty.

Tips from a local guide

  • Walk the ruins first, in the morning light, before the gorge.
  • Water levels swing hard with the season, check before you commit to a swim day.
  • Pairs perfectly with Al Hoota Cave and Misfat in one Nizwa day.

What to bring

  • Water shoes
  • Water
  • Sun hat, the gorge is exposed early on

Good to have

  • Goggles
  • Camera for the ruins
  • Picnic

Wadi Tanuf: questions people ask

When is the best time to visit Wadi Tanuf?

Oct–Apr. Oman's summer (roughly May to September) is extremely hot inland, so plan outdoor places for the cooler months and go early in the day.

How much does Wadi Tanuf cost?

Free.

How long do you need at Wadi Tanuf?

Half day.

How long is the walk at Wadi Tanuf?

30–60 min into the gorge, as far as you fancy.

Can you swim at Wadi Tanuf?

Yes. 30–60 min in the pools when water levels are up.

Do you need a 4x4 to reach Wadi Tanuf?

2WD to the ruins; 4×4 for the rough track further in.

Is Wadi Tanuf good for kids?

Yes, it works with children.
