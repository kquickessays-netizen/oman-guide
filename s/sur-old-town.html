Sur old town & corniche

The dhow-building capital, still building them.

Sur curls around a lagoon where wooden dhows have been launched for a thousand years. Whitewashed lanes, watchtowers, the Bilad Sur Castle, the Al Ayjah lighthouse walk, and a corniche made for slow evenings. The proper overnight stop before or after Ras Al Jinz.

Best forThe evening stroll + dhow heritage
Time neededHalf day or an overnight
Drive2.5–3 hrs from Muscat
Pairs withRas Al Jinz turtles, 40 min on
EntryTown free, castle small fee

How to get to Sur old town & corniche

  1. Route 17 all the way down the coast from Muscat, 2.5–3 hrs, tarmac throughout.
  2. Park by the corniche and do the town on foot, cross to Al Ayjah by the bridge for the lighthouse side.

What you'll do

  1. Walk the corniche at golden hour with the lagoon full of moored dhows.
  2. Look across to Al Ayjah's watchtowers and lighthouse, the classic Sur view.
  3. Poke around Bilad Sur Castle if it's open when you pass.
