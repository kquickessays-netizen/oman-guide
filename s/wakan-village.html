Wakan Village

700 steps up through the orchards, and the blossoms in spring.

A tiny terraced village hanging 2,000m up the wall of Wadi Mistal, stone steps climbing through apricot and pomegranate gardens to a viewpoint over the whole valley. In late February the orchards blossom white and pink, and half of Oman drives up to see it.
