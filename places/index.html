Places to visit in Oman

67 wadis, beaches, mountains, forts, souqs and places to eat, from a licensed Omani tour guide.

Wadis in Oman (13)

  • Wadi Bani Khalid, The face of wadis in Oman.
  • Wadi Shab, The famous one, and for once the hype is real.
  • Wadi Al Abyad, The white wadi, milky-blue pools an hour from Muscat.
  • Wadi Al Arbeieen, One of the best wadis for photoshoots in all of Oman.
  • Wadi Al Khoudh, Muscat's own wadi, one of the closest to drive to.
  • Wadi As Suwayh, Where emerald pools meet rugged cliffs.
  • Wadi Dayqah Dam, The easy family day, kayaks, not scrambling.
  • Wadi Hawer, For people who want an adventure, not a viewpoint.
  • Wadi Tanuf, A ruined village, a gorge, and almost nobody there.
  • Snake Gorge (Wadi Bani Awf), The most adrenaline you'll find in an Omani wadi.
  • Wadi Al Hoqain, the trenches, Sulfur-blue water in carved rock channels, and it's easy if you can swim.
  • Wadi Damm, Holds water all year, almost no wadi here does.
  • Wadi Mibam, The most beautiful wadi in Oman — the top of the Wadi Tiwi valley.

Beaches in Oman (13)

  • Marjan Beach (Ras Al Hamra), Turtles grazing metres from the sand, inside the city, for free.
  • Qurum Beach, The city's beach, and better than a city beach has any right to be.
  • Sidab & its hidden coves, The 100/10 coves. My favourite hike in Muscat, with some of the most beautiful views.
  • As Sifah, A long, pretty, empty stretch an hour from the city.
  • Fins Beach, White sand, turquoise water, mountains behind.
  • Pebble Beach, Tiwi, The relaxed stop on the Tiwi coast everyone drives straight past.
  • Qantab & its ten beaches, One fishing village, ten beaches, three rials.
  • Ras Al Hadd beach camp, Camp at the easternmost tip of Arabia, first sunrise in the Arab world.
  • Sifah Beach, The easy wild beach an hour from town — with a famous surprise in the shallows.
  • Yiti Beach, The easy bay 30–45 minutes from the city.
  • Ras Al Jinz, One of the rarest turtle experiences globally — watch sea turtle species only found in Oman.
  • Al Bustan Beach, A mountain-framed bay ten minutes past Old Muscat.
  • Bandar Khayran, The snorkel and kayak playground near Muscat.

Mountains in Oman (4)

  • Jabal Akhdar, Rose terraces, cold air, and villages hanging off a cliff.
  • Misfat Al Abriyeen, A mud-brick village in the mountains that time forgot to ruin.
  • Wakan Village, 700 steps up through the orchards, and the blossoms in spring.
  • The Mutrah ridge treks, Real mountain trails that start where the city parking ends.

Forts, souqs and things to do in Oman (20)

  • Bimmah Sinkhole, A natural blue swimming hole right off the coast road.
  • Mutrah Souq & Corniche, Old Muscat, best at dusk.
  • Royal Opera House Muscat, Arabia's opera house, worth seeing even with no ticket.
  • Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, The one thing every visitor should do in Muscat.
  • Dhow cruise from Bandar Al Rowdha, Muscat's coast the way it was meant to be seen, from a wooden deck.
  • Hijrat Al Sheikh, A 300-year-old village where the falaj still runs the show.
  • Matrah Fort & the corniche, The little fort with the best free view in old Muscat.
  • Museum of the Frankincense Land, The museum inside Al Baleed that explains the whole south.
  • Old Muscat & Al Alam Palace, The Sultan's palace, two Portuguese forts and the oldest quarter in the capital.
  • Oman Across Ages Museum, The billion-dollar museum in the middle of the country, worth the detour.
  • Rose season on Jabal Akhdar, Three weeks a year, the Green Mountain turns pink.
  • Sur old town & corniche, The dhow-building capital, still building them.
  • The dhow yard at Sur, Watch thousand-year-old shipbuilding happen in front of you.
  • The Romantic Cave, Tiwi, Beautiful enough to name, dangerous enough to respect.
  • Daymaniyat Islands, Oman's best reef, a boat ride from the city.
  • Wahiba Sands, A night in the dunes you won't get back home.
  • Ain Al Kasfah hot springs, Rustaq's natural hot bath, and your post-wadi recovery plan.
  • Bioluminescence at Qantab, The night the sea glows blue. Sometimes.
  • Birkat Al Mouz, Ruins, banana groves and a UNESCO falaj, the gateway to Jabal Akhdar.
  • Whale shark season, Summer's consolation prize: the biggest fish on Earth, off Muscat.

Where to eat in Oman (10)

  • Azura, The Coffee Company, The roastery. For people who care about the bean.
  • Bait Al Luban, Shuwa, with a view of the harbour.
  • Batch, Al Khoud's cute one. Also has a branch in Mall of Oman.
  • Bin Ateeq, Eat on the floor, like you're meant to.
  • Café Farah, Coffee on the sand at Azaiba.
  • La Miel Specialty Coffee, The pre-wadi flat white.
  • Najmat Sur, The 2-rial lunch of the Wadi Shab day.
  • Omani halwa, Mutrah Souq, Watch them stir it in the copper pot.
  • Qaha Specialty Coffee, Omani coffee culture, modernised.
  • Rozna, Dinner inside a fort. The design does half the hosting.

Souqs and shopping in Oman (7)

  • City Centre Muscat, Closest big mall to the airport.
  • Mall of Oman, The biggest one, with an indoor snow park.
  • Mutrah Souq, The oldest souq in Oman, go at dusk.
  • Oman Avenues Mall, Central, calm and easy.
  • Seeb Souq, Where Muscat actually shops, fish, dates and zero tourists.
  • Al Sharaa Mills & Dates, The shop I send everyone to before they fly home.
  • Amouage Visitor Centre, Oman's world-famous perfume house, at the source.
